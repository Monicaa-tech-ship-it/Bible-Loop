Jesus Walks on Water

After feeding the five thousand, Jesus sent the disciples ahead by boat while He went up a mountain to pray alone. Late that night, a storm battered their small vessel far from shore. Through the wind and waves, they saw a figure walking toward them on the water — and were terrified, thinking it was a ghost.
